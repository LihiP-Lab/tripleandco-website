The story

Why Triple exists.

Lihi Pinto spent 15+ years inside B2B companies: investment banking first, then SaaS. She led marketing through fundraises that brought in over $70M, and tripled revenue at a SaaS company. Twice.

Along the way she kept hitting the same wall. Early-stage companies need senior marketing judgment and serious execution capacity, but can only afford one of them. So they hire an agency that ships assets without strategy, or a fractional CMO who writes strategy nobody executes.

Then AI changed the math. Suddenly one senior operator could supervise a team of specialist agents and deliver both: the judgment of an executive and the output of a department. Triple & Co. is that idea, built and running.

"Building the marketing team I wish I had as an early-stage founder."

Today Triple works with B2B companies in Israel and globally. About half the work is international. The model is simple: Lihi owns the strategy and the outcome, eight supervised AI agents handle the volume, and nothing ships without human review.
