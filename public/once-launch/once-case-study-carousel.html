How we did it
Slide 2 · The situation
01 / The situation
The brief

A Series B startup, ~80 people, had just lost its VP R&D.

The board meeting was eight weeks out. Two agencies had quoted "three to four months." That timeline didn't fit the reality of the business.
Slide 3 · Calibrate
02 / Days 1–2
Calibrate, don't source

We didn't start by sourcing. We started by getting the target right.

The last VP had failed on the jump from hands-on builder to leader of leaders, not on technical ability. So that became the thing we hired against. A sharp target is what lets a search move fast.
Slide 4 · Map the market
03 / Days 2–6
Map the whole market

The entire landscape, mapped early, including the people who'd never appear in a job search.

Our sourcing agent did the heavy lifting; the founders aimed judgment at the shortlist. Searches take months because they're done a few names at a time. Mapping the whole market early collapses that.
Slide 5 · Founder-led + close
04 / Days 6–11
Founder-led outreach

Every approach drafted for the person. Sent under a founder's name.

A VP-level candidate responds to seriousness, not a template. The first conversation was with Inbal, not a coordinator. A well-aimed search isn't 100 CVs. It's a handful of exactly-right people in real dialogue.
Every search ends once.
